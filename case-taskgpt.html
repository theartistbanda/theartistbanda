TaskGPT: the AI that works inside the call

An enterprise AI suite for agents on live customer calls, and for the people who set up, measure and improve the AI behind them. I was the only designer, from 2022 to 2025. All 11 products and 7 Chrome extensions went live.

The problem

Before TaskGPT, agents did this work by hand. They kept several portals, sheets and documents open, and searched them during the call for the right answer. My first job was in a call centre, so I know what that costs: a pause the customer hears.

TaskUs serves many Fortune 500 tech companies. Each one has its own product, its own rules for refunds and returns, and its own picture of the solution. One multi-tenant product had to serve all of them.

From one chatbot to a suite

Within a week of OpenAI's ChatGPT launch, we demoed our own AI chatbot to a client. It put each client's knowledge at the agent's fingertips, answered from that client's own knowledge base, and AHT dropped quickly. Then managers, sales teams and team leads started to use it, and soon every campaign wanted TaskGPT. One chatbot grew into 11 products and 7 browser extensions.

One real moment

Near the end of a quarter, our SVP asked me how we could show clients, in simple numbers, how TaskGPT helped them. My manager wanted depth. The development manager wanted the code on screen. I designed for one moment instead: our SVP on a client call. The first screen showed only the primary numbers, and the depth sat one level down for whoever needed it. The next quarter, TaskGPT sold even faster.

Outcome

All 11 products and 7 extensions went live, with call-handling time and operational costs each coming down 20%. After the commercial revamp, adoption rose by an average of 12%. Because measurement was part of the design, the team could see adoption, answer quality and token cost side by side, and decide where to invest next.

What I learned: put help where the work is, show what the AI did, and design for the next decision, not the data.
