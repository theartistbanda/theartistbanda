TranslateBuddy

From a standalone translator to a Zendesk panel that detects the language and prepares the reply, so agents only review, edit and send.

  • Lead Product Designer

TaskGPT is the AI suite I designed at TaskUs for agents on live customer calls, and for the people who set up, measure and improve the AI behind them. I was the only designer, from 2022 to 2025. TranslateBuddy is one of its 7 Chrome extensions.

See all of TaskGPTRead the full case study

Who it is for
Agents on tickets in other languages
Where it runs
Browser, then Zendesk chat and email
First date in the file
2022

Agents on international customer support answer customers in languages they do not speak. TranslateBuddy started as a standalone translator in the browser. It was more manual, but it already helped with multilingual conversations: an on-select mode, a clipboard of recent translations, a confidence score, a rating, and a note that accuracy drops when neither language is English. Then I designed it into Zendesk. There it detects the customer's language, translates the message, and prepares the answer, so the agent only reviews, edits and pastes it into the chat. Agents worked faster. The email version adds translated subject lines, translation of a selected part only, "Show original", and a transcript and summary of the whole thread in both languages, side by side.

Design decisions

  • Go to the work. Each extension sits beside the page the person already uses, so nobody changes tabs in the middle of a ticket.
  • Show confidence. Translation and deepfake results carry a score, so people know how far to trust them.
  • Ask the person to confirm. Co-Pilot, TranslateBuddy and Deepfake Detection all ask for a rating or a check, and that feedback improves the AI.
