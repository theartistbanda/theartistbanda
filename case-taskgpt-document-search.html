Document Search

Questions across long documents

One question across more than 50 long client documents. Each answer comes with its reasoning and citation, and long searches run in a queue while people carry on.

  • Only designer
  • TaskUs
  • 2022 to 2025

TaskGPT is the AI suite I designed at TaskUs for agents on live customer calls, and for the people who set up, measure and improve the AI behind them. I was the only designer, from 2022 to 2025. Document Search is one of its 11 products.

See all of TaskGPTRead the full case study

Ask one question across a client's whole document library, and get an answer for each file with its reasoning and citation.

The story

It began as a proof of concept for the sales team, and went live after the proof of concept worked. A lead who manages several top logos came to Digital IT with a problem. From cold lead to warm lead to signed client, the same questions come up in meeting after meeting. Everyone involved in onboarding has to answer them again, from more than 50 long documents, with KPI data and citations. Each client call also needed the people who knew those details on the call.

They did not bring a solution, only how they worked. I joined a call with my managers to see it. They used several sheets and the find function to hunt for a term across many documents, in as few tries as they could. I also saw that they kept the files in Google Drive, so I proposed a Google Drive connector.

What I designed

  • One question field. People pick a saved question or write a new one, then choose the clients to search. Version 4 adds a list of processed clients and quick chips to add a new client.
  • Results for each file. Client, path link, answer, reasoning and citation, plus a count such as "45/45 file(s) found matching your query". Results export.
  • A queue state. "Your question is in the queue, it will be processed soon", with skeleton cards, because long searches run in the background.
  • Requested Questions. Every question with who asked it, its status (success, in progress with "4/5 files", failed or terminated) and a Terminate action for long runs.
  • Manage document search, for admins. Pick the client and the connector (Google Drive), paste and verify a path link, include sub-folders, and sync now. A Files tab shows the sync status of each file. A Questions tab holds the saved questions.

Design decisions

  • Start from intent. Under the hood the product can do a lot, but people come here to ask a question. So the page opens on one field: a saved question or a new one.
  • The least clicks possible. Pick the clients, then search. The Google Drive connector syncs the files of many clients at once.
  • No waiting. A search goes into a queue, and people carry on with other work while the agent finds the answers.
  • Keep every answer. Answers, reasoning and citations are stored, and people view, share or download them in one click.
  • Check before use. Answer, reasoning and citation sit together, so a person can check the AI before they use the answer.

Later phases planned more analytics and a bulk way to upload questions: upload an Excel file from a template, check its format, show results as they process, and download them.
